# Remote Work Policy

**Version:** 2.0 · **Effective:** 2025-03-01 · **Owner:** People Team · **Applies to:** all employees of Crate Expectations S.L.

## 1. Working model

Crate operates a hybrid model. Eligible employees may work remotely up to **3 days per week** without prior approval, provided the schedule is agreed with their team. The remaining days are spent in the office, with Tuesday and Thursday recommended as common in-office days.

## 2. Eligibility

| Group | Remote days allowed | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Employees after probation | Up to 3 days per week | None beyond team scheduling |
| Employees in onboarding (first 2 months) | Up to 2 days per week | Minimum 3 in-office days per week |
| Employees in probation period | Up to 2 days per week | Manager may request more in-office days for coaching |
| Part-time employees | Pro rata to contracted days | The 3-day limit scales with working days (for example, up to 2 days for a 4-day week) |
| On-site roles (office reception, warehouse, hardware support) | Not applicable | Excluded because the work must be done on site |

### 2.1 Exceptions

A manager may temporarily require full in-office attendance for operational reasons (for example, an incident or an audit) with at least 48 hours' notice, except in emergencies. Temporary requirements longer than 4 weeks must be agreed with the People Team.

## 3. Expense compensation

| Item | Amount | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly remote work allowance (electricity, internet) | EUR 55 per month | Paid with payroll to employees who work remotely at least 8 days per month on a quarterly average |
| Ergonomic furniture, one-off | Up to EUR 300 | Once every 3 years, with receipt |
| External monitor and peripherals | Provided by Crate | Request through the IT service desk |
| Corporate laptop | Provided by Crate | Mandatory for all remote work |

The allowance is pro-rated for part-time employees. It is not paid during months of unpaid leave. The allowance and one-off payments are subject to payroll tax rules in force; Crate will indicate the taxable portion on the payslip.

## 4. Working from abroad

| Destination | Allowance per year | Approval |
|---|---|---|
| Spain (other than usual work location) | Unlimited within the remote day limit | None |
| Other EU or EEA country | Up to 20 working days | Prior People Team approval; at least 15 days' notice |
| Outside the EU or EEA | Not permitted | Executive approval only, in exceptional cases |

Working from abroad can create tax, social security and immigration obligations for both the employee and Crate. Approval is not granted for destinations where Crate has no entity and the stay would exceed 20 working days, or where the employee does not have the right to work.

## 5. Security requirements

1. Use the corporate laptop and the corporate VPN for any access to internal systems.
2. Do not work on confidential documents in public spaces without a privacy screen.
3. Lock the device whenever you step away.
4. Report loss or theft of any device to security@crateexpectations.example immediately, and in any case within 1 hour of noticing.
5. Do not use public or shared computers to access Crate systems.

## 6. Availability and working hours

Employees stay reachable during the core hours of **10:00 to 16:00** (Madrid time). Outside core hours, working time is flexible within contracted hours. Remote days follow the same working time and rest rules as in-office days.

## 7. Health and safety

Employees are responsible for a workspace that meets basic ergonomic standards. Crate provides a self-assessment checklist and, on request, an ergonomic review by the occupational health service.

## 8. Ending or changing arrangements

Either the employee or Crate may end an individual remote arrangement with 15 days' notice if it is not working. Crate will give the reasons in writing on request.
