# Remote Work Policy

**Version:** 1.0 · **Effective:** 2022-09-01 · **Owner:** People Team · **Applies to:** all employees of Crate Expectations S.L.

## 1. Working model

Remote work is an exceptional and voluntary arrangement. Eligible employees may work remotely up to **2 days per week**, with the **written authorisation of their line manager**, which must be renewed every quarter.

## 2. Eligibility

| Group | Remote days allowed | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Employees on permanent contracts with more than 6 months' service | Up to 2 days per week | Written manager authorisation, renewed quarterly |
| Employees on fixed-term contracts | Up to 2 days per week | Authorisation from both the manager and the People Team |
| Employees in their first 6 months | None | Fully in the office |
| On-site roles | Not applicable | Excluded |

## 3. Expense compensation

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Monthly remote work allowance | EUR 30 per month |
| Ergonomic furniture | Not compensated |
| Corporate laptop | Provided by Crate |

The allowance is paid with payroll to employees who work remotely at least 4 days per month.

## 4. Information security

1. The corporate VPN must be used for all access to internal systems.
2. Company information must not be stored on personal devices.
3. Loss or theft of a device must be reported to the IT service desk on the same working day.

## 5. Working from abroad

Remote work is only permitted from within Spain. Working from any other country is not allowed.

## 6. Working hours

On remote days the employee follows their usual working hours of 09:00 to 18:00 and must be reachable throughout the working day.

## 7. Ending the arrangement

The line manager may withdraw authorisation at any time with 5 working days' notice if the arrangement affects the team's performance.
