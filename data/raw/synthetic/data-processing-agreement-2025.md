# Data Processing Agreement

**Reference:** DPA-2025-014 · **Date:** 2025-07-01 · **Schedule D to MSA-2025-014** · **Classification:** Confidential

**Controller:** Nortecloud Retail S.A. · **Processor:** Crate Expectations S.L.

## 1. Subject matter and roles

The Controller determines the purposes and means of processing personal data contained in Customer Data. The Processor processes that data only on behalf of the Controller to provide the Services under MSA-2025-014.

## 2. Description of processing

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| Nature and purpose | Hosting and operation of the Controller's e-commerce platform |
| Duration | The term of MSA-2025-014 and the return or deletion period in section 9 |
| Types of personal data | Identification data, contact data, order history, delivery addresses, hashed account credentials |
| Data subjects | The Controller's customers and website visitors; the Controller's employees who use the Platform |
| Special categories | None. The Controller must not upload special category data without prior written agreement |

## 3. Processor obligations

1. Process personal data only on documented instructions from the Controller, including regarding transfers outside the EEA, unless required by law (in which case the Processor informs the Controller beforehand, where permitted).
2. Ensure that persons authorised to process the data are bound by confidentiality.
3. Implement the security measures in section 4.
4. Respect the conditions for engaging sub-processors in section 5.
5. Assist the Controller with data subject requests, impact assessments and consultations with the supervisory authority.
6. Make available the information needed to demonstrate compliance and allow audits under section 8.
7. Inform the Controller immediately if an instruction appears to infringe data protection law.

## 4. Security measures

| Area | Measure |
|---|---|
| Encryption in transit | TLS 1.2 or higher on all connections |
| Encryption at rest | AES-256 for databases and backups |
| Access control | Role-based access, named accounts, multi-factor authentication for administrative access |
| Logging | Access to personal data logged and retained for 13 months |
| Segregation | Logical separation of Customer Data from other customers' data |
| Resilience | Hourly backups, quarterly restore tests, RPO 1 hour, RTO 4 hours |
| Testing | Annual third-party penetration test; summary shared with the Controller on request |
| Personnel | Background checks for staff with production access; annual data protection training |

## 5. Sub-processors

The Controller gives general authorisation for the sub-processors below.

| Sub-processor | Service | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Hosting Iberia S.L. | Server infrastructure | Spain |
| BackupSafe GmbH | Encrypted backup storage | Germany |
| MailRelay Ltd | Transactional email delivery | Ireland |

The Processor informs the Controller **30 days** before adding or replacing a sub-processor. The Controller may object on reasonable grounds within that period; if the parties cannot agree, the Controller may terminate the affected Services without penalty. The Processor imposes on each sub-processor obligations equivalent to this agreement and remains liable for them.

## 6. Personal data breaches

6.1 The Processor notifies the Controller **without undue delay and in any case within 24 hours** of becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Customer Data.
6.2 The notification includes, as far as known: the nature of the breach, categories and approximate number of data subjects and records, likely consequences, and measures taken or proposed.
6.3 Information not yet available may be provided in phases. The Processor does not notify supervisory authorities or data subjects itself unless the Controller instructs it to or the law requires it.
6.4 The Controller is responsible for notifying the supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach, where required.

## 7. Data subject requests

The Processor forwards any request it receives from a data subject to the Controller within **2 working days** and does not respond itself unless instructed. The Processor assists the Controller with requests within **5 working days** of the Controller's instruction.

## 8. Audits

The Controller may audit the Processor's compliance once per year with **20 days'** notice, or at any time following a personal data breach. Audits are carried out during business hours, without disrupting the Processor's operations, and are subject to confidentiality. The Processor may satisfy the audit right by providing recent independent audit reports where these cover the same scope.

## 9. Return and deletion

At the end of the Services, the Processor returns Customer Data in a standard machine-readable format within **30 days** and deletes it from its systems within **90 days** after that, including from backups as they expire. The Processor certifies deletion in writing.

## 10. International transfers

No transfer of personal data outside the EEA takes place without the Controller's prior written consent. Where consent is given, the parties use standard contractual clauses.

## 11. Liability

Liability under this agreement follows the limits in clause 9 of MSA-2025-014, except that those limits do not apply to liability arising from breach of sections 3 and 6 of this agreement caused by gross negligence.
