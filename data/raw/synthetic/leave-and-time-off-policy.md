# Leave and Time Off Policy

**Version:** 1.4 · **Effective:** 2026-01-01 · **Owner:** People Team · **Applies to:** all employees in Spain

## 1. Annual leave

Every employee is entitled to **24 working days** of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to national and local public holidays. Leave accrues in proportion to time worked during the year.

- Requests are submitted in the employee portal at least **15 days** in advance.
- The line manager answers within 5 working days; if there is no answer, the request is escalated to the People Team.
- At least **10 working days** of the annual leave must be taken consecutively.
- Up to **5 days** may be carried over to the first quarter of the next year; remaining days not taken are lost, except where the employee was prevented from taking them by sick leave or statutory leave.

### 1.1 Additional days for length of service

| Length of service | Additional days |
|---|---|
| Less than 3 years | 0 |
| 3 to under 5 years | 1 |
| 5 to under 10 years | 2 |
| 10 years or more | 3 |

Additional days are granted from January 1 of the year in which the threshold is reached.

### 1.2 Joining or leaving during the year

Leave is pro-rated to the whole months worked. On leaving, unused days are paid in the final payslip; days taken in excess of the accrued amount are deducted, except in cases of redundancy.

## 2. Paid special leave

| Reason | Duration |
|---|---|
| Marriage or registered partnership | 15 calendar days |
| Birth or adoption (second parent, in addition to statutory leave) | 2 weeks |
| Death of spouse, parent or child | 5 working days |
| Death of another relative up to second degree | 3 working days (5 if travel of more than 300 km is required) |
| Moving the main residence | 1 working day |
| Medical appointments with proof | The time needed |
| Official exams | The day of the exam |
| Serious illness or hospitalisation of a close relative | 3 working days |

Special leave is counted in working days and begins on the first working day after the event, unless the event occurs on a working day, in which case it begins that day.

## 3. Unpaid leave

Up to **30 calendar days per year** of unpaid leave for personal reasons may be granted, subject to approval by the line manager and the People Team. Unpaid leave of more than 15 days suspends the accrual of annual leave for the period. Health insurance continues during unpaid leave if the employee pays their share.

## 4. Sick leave

1. Notify the line manager on the first day, before 10:00.
2. Submit the medical certificate within **3 days**.
3. Crate tops up the statutory sickness benefit to **100 % of fixed salary for the first 3 months** of any single period of sick leave, and to 75 % thereafter up to 12 months.
4. Sick leave during annual leave suspends the leave if certified by a doctor from the start of the illness; the days are returned to the employee's balance.

## 5. Parental leave

Statutory parental leave applies. In addition, Crate offers flexible return arrangements for 6 months after the end of statutory leave, such as a reduced schedule at full pay for the first 4 weeks.

## 6. Public holiday periods

August 15 and the Christmas week are reduced-activity periods. Employees are encouraged to plan leave then; the team must keep minimum cover agreed with the manager.
