# Travel and Expenses Policy

**Version:** 3.1 · **Effective:** 2026-02-01 · **Owner:** Finance · **Applies to:** all employees and contractors travelling on behalf of Crate Expectations S.L.

## 1. Principles

Expenses must be necessary for the business, reasonable and supported by an itemised receipt. Expenses without receipts are not reimbursed, except for small amounts under EUR 15 where a receipt cannot be obtained, up to EUR 60 per month.

## 2. Limits by category

| Category | Limit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Accommodation, Spain | EUR 130 per night | Up to EUR 180 in Madrid and Barcelona |
| Accommodation, rest of Europe | EUR 160 per night | Up to EUR 200 in capital cities |
| Accommodation, outside Europe | EUR 220 per night | Requires prior approval for stays over 5 nights |
| Meals | EUR 25 per meal | Maximum 2 meals per day; no alcohol |
| Taxi or ride-hailing | Only when public transport is not reasonable | State the reason on the expense line |
| Air travel | Economy class | Flights over 6 hours: premium economy allowed |
| Rail | Standard class | High-speed rail allowed |
| Own car | EUR 0.26 per km | Supported by route |
| Rental car | Compact class | Only if cheaper than alternatives over the trip |

## 3. Approval levels

| Total trip cost | Approval required |
|---|---|
| Up to EUR 500 | Line manager |
| EUR 500 to EUR 2,000 | Line manager and area director |
| Over EUR 2,000 | Area director and finance director |

International trips outside the EU always require prior approval, regardless of cost. Bookings should be made through the corporate travel tool at least 14 days in advance; bookings made later than that may be refused for fares above the average booked fare for the route.

## 4. Reimbursement

1. Submit expenses in the expense tool within **30 days** of incurring them.
2. Expenses submitted after 60 days are reimbursed only with finance director approval.
3. Approved expenses are paid with the next payroll after approval.
4. Foreign currency expenses are converted at the card statement rate or, for cash, at the daily rate of the European Central Bank.

## 5. Non-reimbursable items

Alcohol outside business dinners, fines and penalties, minibar, gym, personal entertainment, personal travel insurance, and any expense of accompanying persons.

## 6. Client entertainment

| Item | Limit |
|---|---|
| Client meal | EUR 60 per person |
| Events and tickets | EUR 120 per person; director approval required |

Claims must list the names of attendees and the business purpose.
