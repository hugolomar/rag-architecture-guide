# Supplier Code of Conduct

**Version:** 2.1 · **Effective:** 2024-09-01 · **Owner:** Compliance Office, Crate Expectations S.L. · **Applies to:** all suppliers, contractors and their subcontractors

## 1. Purpose and scope

This Code sets out the standards Crate Expectations S.L. ("Crate") expects from every organisation that supplies it with goods or services. Acceptance of this Code is a condition for appearing on the Approved Supplier Register. It applies to the supplier's own staff and to any subcontractor the supplier uses to deliver work for Crate.

### 1.1 Definitions

- **Supplier**: any legal entity that provides goods or services to Crate under a purchase order or contract.
- **Subcontractor**: a third party engaged by a Supplier to perform part of the work for Crate.
- **Public official**: any person holding a legislative, administrative or judicial office, or employed by a state-owned entity.
- **Gift**: anything of value offered without payment of market price, including meals, travel, tickets and discounts.

## 2. Core principles

1. **Legal compliance.** Suppliers comply with all applicable labour, tax, environmental and data protection law.
2. **Integrity.** Suppliers never offer or accept bribes, kickbacks or facilitation payments.
3. **Information security.** Suppliers protect Crate information with appropriate technical and organisational measures (see section 6).
4. **Fair labour.** Child labour and forced labour are prohibited; legal minimum wages and maximum working hours are respected.
5. **Sustainability.** Suppliers disclose their carbon footprint on request and work to reduce it.

## 3. Gifts and hospitality

| Situation | Rule |
|---|---|
| Gift worth less than EUR 50 | Allowed, no disclosure needed |
| Gift worth EUR 50 to EUR 150 | Must be disclosed to the Compliance Office within 5 working days |
| Gift worth more than EUR 150 | Not allowed; must be declined or returned |
| Meals and events with a business purpose | Allowed if reasonable; the supplier and the Crate employee must both be present |
| Gifts to or from a public official | Never allowed, regardless of value |
| Cash or cash equivalents (vouchers, gift cards) | Never allowed |

The thresholds apply per person per calendar year in aggregate: three gifts of EUR 40 from the same supplier count as EUR 120 and fall into the disclosure band.

## 4. Conflicts of interest

A Supplier must disclose any situation in which an Crate employee involved in the relationship has a financial interest in the Supplier, or a close family relationship with its owners or management. Disclosure is made to the Compliance Office before the contract is signed or, if the situation arises later, within 10 working days of becoming aware of it.

## 5. Subcontracting

1. Subcontractors must be approved in writing by Crate before they begin work.
2. The Supplier remains fully responsible for the subcontractor's compliance with this Code.
3. Where the subcontractor will process personal data, a data processing agreement must be in place before any data is shared.

## 6. Information security requirements

| Requirement | Minimum standard |
|---|---|
| Access control | Named accounts, multi-factor authentication for any access to Crate systems |
| Encryption | TLS 1.2 or higher in transit; AES-256 or equivalent at rest |
| Incident notification | Notify Crate within 48 hours of becoming aware of an incident affecting Crate information (24 hours where a data processing agreement sets a shorter period) |
| Return and deletion | Return or securely delete Crate information within 30 days of contract end |
| Background checks | Required for staff with access to production systems |

## 7. Audit rights

Crate may audit a Supplier's compliance with this Code once every 12 months with 20 days' notice, and at any time with 5 days' notice after a reported incident or credible allegation of breach. Audits are conducted during business hours and are limited to information relevant to the services.

## 8. Reporting concerns

Concerns can be reported, anonymously if preferred, to ethics@crateexpectations.example. Crate prohibits retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith.

## 9. Consequences of breach

| Severity | Examples | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Minor | Late disclosure of a gift in the EUR 50 to EUR 150 band | Written reminder |
| Serious | Unapproved subcontracting; missed incident notification | Corrective action plan within 30 days; temporary suspension from new work |
| Critical | Bribery, falsified records, deliberate data misuse | Removal from the Approved Supplier Register and termination of contracts |
