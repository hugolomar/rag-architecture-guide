# Salary Bands 2026

**Version:** 2026.1 · **Effective:** 2026-01-01 · **Classification:** Restricted. People Team and executive leadership only

## 1. Structure

Every role is assigned to one band according to its level of responsibility. Amounts are gross annual base salary in euros, for full-time work in Spain.

## 2. Bands

| Band | Level | Minimum | Midpoint | Maximum | Range spread |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | Junior / Associate | 24,000 | 28,000 | 32,000 | 33 % |
| B2 | Professional | 32,000 | 38,000 | 44,000 | 38 % |
| B3 | Senior | 44,000 | 52,000 | 60,000 | 36 % |
| B4 | Lead / Principal specialist | 58,000 | 67,000 | 76,000 | 31 % |
| B5 | Manager | 70,000 | 80,000 | 90,000 | 29 % |
| B6 | Director | 88,000 | 102,000 | 116,000 | 32 % |

Adjacent bands overlap deliberately, which allows a strong performer in one band to earn more than a newly promoted colleague in the next.

## 3. Specialty premiums

A premium is added to the base salary for scarce skills. Premiums are applied to the band amounts and do not change the band itself.

| Specialty | Premium |
|---|---|
| Security and cybersecurity | +8 % |
| Data and AI | +6 % |
| Cloud infrastructure and site reliability | +5 % |
| Other technical specialties | 0 % |
| Non-technical roles | 0 % |

## 4. Location differential

| Work location | Differential |
|---|---|
| Madrid, Barcelona | 0 % (reference) |
| Other cities in Spain | -5 % |
| Fully remote outside the above | -5 % |

## 5. Rules for offers and changes

1. **New hires** enter between the minimum and the midpoint of their band. An offer above the midpoint requires approval from the People Director.
2. **No salary exceeds the band maximum** (before premium). Employees reaching the maximum receive increases as non-consolidated payments until promoted.
3. **Annual review** increases range from 0 % to **4 %**, determined by the performance rating (see the Bonus and Performance Plan 2026) and position in the range; employees rated 3 or above receive between 2 % and 4 %, as shown in section 6.
4. **Promotion to the next band** carries a minimum increase of **8 %**, and the new salary must be at least the minimum of the new band.
5. **Counter-offers** to retain an employee require approval from the People Director and the relevant executive, and cannot exceed the band maximum plus premium.

## 6. Increase guideline by rating and range position

| Rating | Below midpoint | At or above midpoint |
|---|---|---|
| 5: Exceptional | 4.0 % | 3.5 % |
| 4: Exceeds expectations | 3.5 % | 3.0 % |
| 3: Meets expectations | 3.0 % | 2.0 % |
| 2: Partially meets | 1.0 % | 0 % |
| 1: Does not meet | 0 % | 0 % |

## 7. Worked example

A candidate for a Senior (B3) site reliability engineer position based in Valencia:

- Band B3 midpoint: EUR 52,000. Entry salary may be set between EUR 44,000 and EUR 52,000.
- Location differential: -5 % applied to the reference range, giving EUR 41,800 to EUR 49,400.
- Specialty premium: +5 % for cloud infrastructure and site reliability.
- Example offer: EUR 46,000 base, plus premium of EUR 2,300, for a total fixed salary of **EUR 48,300**.

## 8. Confidentiality

These bands must not be shared with employees or candidates. Candidates receive only the range of their specific offer. Managers may discuss their own team members' salaries only with the People Team.
