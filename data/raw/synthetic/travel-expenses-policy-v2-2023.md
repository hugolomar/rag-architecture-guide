# Travel and Expenses Policy

**Version:** 2.2 · **Effective:** 2023-04-01 · **Owner:** Finance · **Applies to:** all employees travelling on behalf of Crate Expectations S.L.

## 1. Principles

Expenses must be necessary for the business, reasonable and supported by an itemised receipt. Expenses without receipts are not reimbursed.

## 2. Limits by category

| Category | Limit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Accommodation, Spain | EUR 110 per night | Up to EUR 150 in Madrid and Barcelona |
| Accommodation, rest of Europe | EUR 140 per night | Up to EUR 170 in capital cities |
| Meals | EUR 20 per meal | Maximum 2 meals per day |
| Taxi or ride-hailing | Only when public transport is not reasonable | |
| Air travel | Economy class | No exceptions |
| Rail | Standard class | |
| Own car | EUR 0.22 per km | |

## 3. Approval levels

| Total trip cost | Approval required |
|---|---|
| Up to EUR 400 | Line manager |
| EUR 400 to EUR 1,500 | Line manager and area director |
| Over EUR 1,500 | Area director and finance director |

All international trips require prior approval.

## 4. Reimbursement

Expenses must be submitted in the expense tool within **21 days** of being incurred. Approved expenses are paid with the next payroll.

## 5. Non-reimbursable items

Alcohol, fines, minibar, gym, personal entertainment and expenses of accompanying persons.
