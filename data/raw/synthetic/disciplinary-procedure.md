# Disciplinary Procedure

**Version:** 2.0 · **Effective:** 2025-01-15 · **Classification:** Restricted. People Team, Legal and executive leadership only

## 1. Purpose

This procedure sets out how Crate Expectations S.L. investigates and sanctions breaches of employment duties, guaranteeing the employee's right to be heard and the proportionality of any sanction. It is applied in accordance with applicable labour law and the collective agreement.

## 2. Classification of misconduct

| Category | Examples | Possible sanction |
|---|---|---|
| Minor | Repeated lateness; careless use of equipment; late submission of records | Verbal or written warning |
| Serious | 3 unjustified absences within 30 days; breach of information security rules (for example, sharing credentials); failure to report a security incident within the required time | Suspension without pay of up to 15 days |
| Very serious | Fraud; harassment; leaking confidential information; unjustified absences exceeding 10 days in 6 months; working while on sick leave in a way that contradicts the medical certificate | Suspension of up to 60 days or dismissal |

Repeated minor misconduct (three written warnings within 12 months) may be treated as serious. Repeated serious misconduct within 12 months may be treated as very serious.

## 3. Principles

1. Employees are presumed to have acted correctly until a decision is made.
2. The investigation is confidential; only people who need to know are informed.
3. The employee may be accompanied at the hearing by a workers' representative.
4. The sanction is proportionate to the facts, the intent, the harm caused and the employee's record.
5. No one who raises a concern in good faith suffers retaliation.

## 4. Stages

| Stage | What happens | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Report | A manager or any employee reports the incident to the People Team | As soon as possible |
| 2. Preliminary review | People Team decides whether a formal investigation is warranted | 3 working days |
| 3. Investigation | Facts and evidence are gathered; witnesses interviewed; written record kept | 10 working days |
| 4. Hearing | The employee is told of the allegations in writing and invited to respond | Notice of 3 working days before the hearing |
| 5. Decision | The disciplinary committee (People Team, Legal, area director) decides in writing | 10 working days after the hearing |
| 6. Notification | The employee receives a letter with the facts, classification, sanction and effective date | Within 2 working days of the decision |
| 7. Appeal | The employee may appeal to the People Director in writing | 10 working days after notification |

The People Director decides the appeal within 15 working days. The appeal does not suspend a dismissal that has been notified, but the outcome may lead to reinstatement or compensation.

## 5. Evidence handling

Digital evidence (emails, access logs, chat messages) is collected only from company systems, with the involvement of the Security Team, and only to the extent necessary and proportionate. Personal devices and private accounts are not searched. Evidence is stored in the confidential case file and accessed only by the committee.

## 6. Precautionary measures

During the investigation the company may, if necessary to protect people, evidence or operations, reassign duties or place the employee on paid leave for up to 15 working days. This is not a sanction.

## 7. Time limits for sanctions

Sanctions can only be imposed within these periods from the date the company became aware of the misconduct:

| Category | Period |
|---|---|
| Minor | 10 working days |
| Serious | 20 working days |
| Very serious | 60 calendar days |

In any case, misconduct cannot be sanctioned more than 6 months after it was committed.

## 8. Harassment cases

Complaints of harassment follow the specific harassment protocol: immediate protective measures, investigation by independent personnel, and a decision within **30 calendar days**.

## 9. Records

Case files are kept in the confidential People Team archive. Minor sanctions are removed from the employee's record after **12 months** without reoffending; serious sanctions after 24 months.
