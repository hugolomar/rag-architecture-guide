# Master Services Agreement

**Reference:** MSA-2025-014 · **Date:** 2025-07-01 · **Classification:** Confidential

**Parties**

- **Provider:** Crate Expectations S.L. ("Crate")
- **Customer:** Nortecloud Retail S.A. ("the Customer")

**Related documents:** Service Level Agreement SLA-PLAT-2.0 (Schedule C) and Data Processing Agreement DPA-2025-014 (Schedule D).

## 1. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Platform** | The managed e-commerce platform described in Schedule A |
| **Services** | The Platform together with the support, hosting and consultancy services described in Schedule A |
| **Monthly Fee** | The sum of the Base Fee and the Premium Support Fee for a calendar month, before usage-based charges |
| **Initial Term** | The 36-month period starting on the Effective Date (2025-07-01) |
| **Confidential Information** | Non-public information disclosed by one party to the other, in any form, that is marked confidential or should reasonably be understood to be |
| **Customer Data** | Data uploaded to or generated on the Platform by or for the Customer, including personal data |

## 2. Term and renewal

2.1 The agreement starts on the Effective Date and runs for the Initial Term.
2.2 After the Initial Term it renews automatically for successive periods of **12 months** unless either party gives at least **90 days'** written notice before the end of the current period.
2.3 Notice of non-renewal given less than 90 days before the end of the period takes effect at the end of the following period.

## 3. Services and change control

3.1 Crate provides the Services in accordance with Schedule A and the Service Level Agreement.
3.2 Changes to the scope of Services require a written change request signed by both parties. Crate responds to a change request within 10 working days with an impact assessment covering fees, timeline and risk.
3.3 Work performed on a time-and-materials basis is charged at the consultancy rate in Schedule B and requires prior written approval for any estimate above EUR 5,000.

## 4. Fees and payment

### 4.1 Fee schedule (Schedule B)

| Item | Amount | Billing |
|---|---|---|
| Base Fee (Platform) | EUR 18,500 | Monthly |
| Premium Support (24x7) | EUR 3,200 | Monthly |
| Additional storage | EUR 0.09 per GB per month | Monthly, in arrears, on average usage |
| Additional compute bursts | EUR 0.45 per vCPU-hour | Monthly, in arrears |
| Consultancy | EUR 95 per hour | On request, in arrears |
| Onboarding | EUR 12,000 | One-off, invoiced on the Effective Date |

4.2 Invoices are issued monthly and are payable within **45 days** of the invoice date.
4.3 Late payment accrues interest at the statutory commercial rate. Crate may suspend the Services after 30 days' written notice of non-payment of undisputed amounts.
4.4 Fees are reviewed each 1 January in line with the consumer price index (CPI), capped at **4 %** per year. The first review takes place on 2026-01-01.
4.5 Amounts are exclusive of VAT, which is charged where applicable.

## 5. Service levels

The availability commitment, support response times and service credits are set out in the Service Level Agreement (Schedule C). Service credits are the Customer's **sole and exclusive remedy** for unavailability of the Platform, without prejudice to the Customer's right to terminate under clause 11.2.

## 6. Customer Data and data protection

6.1 Customer Data remains the property of the Customer.
6.2 Crate acts as a processor of personal data in Customer Data and processes it under Schedule D.
6.3 Crate implements the security measures listed in Schedule D and notifies the Customer of personal data breaches within the period stated there.

## 7. Confidentiality

7.1 Each party keeps the other's Confidential Information secret and uses it only for the purposes of this agreement.
7.2 The obligation lasts for the term and for **5 years** after the agreement ends. Trade secrets remain protected for as long as they remain secret.
7.3 The obligation does not apply to information that is public through no fault of the recipient, was lawfully known to it, or must be disclosed by law.

## 8. Intellectual property

Each party retains its pre-existing intellectual property. Crate grants the Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to use the Platform during the term. Custom developments paid for by the Customer under a change request are owned by the Customer unless the change request says otherwise; Crate retains ownership of generic tools and know-how.

## 9. Warranties and liability

9.1 Crate warrants that the Services will be provided with reasonable skill and care.
9.2 Crate's total liability under or in connection with this agreement, whether in contract or tort, is limited to the **fees paid or payable in the 12 months** before the event giving rise to the claim.
9.3 Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, including loss of profit, except where caused by wilful misconduct.
9.4 The limit in 9.2 does not apply to liability for wilful misconduct, gross negligence, breach of confidentiality obligations, or liability that cannot be limited by law.
9.5 Crate maintains professional indemnity insurance of at least EUR 3,000,000 per claim during the term.

## 10. Force majeure

Neither party is liable for delay or failure caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it notifies the other promptly and takes reasonable steps to limit the effect. If a force majeure event lasts more than 60 days, either party may terminate without penalty on 15 days' notice.

## 11. Termination

11.1 **For material breach.** Either party may terminate if the other commits a material breach and does not remedy it within **30 days** of written notice.
11.2 **For repeated service failure.** The Customer may terminate without penalty if the availability commitment is missed in three consecutive months, or in any four months in a rolling twelve-month period.
11.3 **Insolvency.** Either party may terminate immediately if the other becomes insolvent.
11.4 **Early termination without cause by the Customer.** The Customer may terminate for convenience on 60 days' notice, subject to the early termination fee below.

| Termination effective in contract month | Early termination fee |
|---|---|
| Months 1 to 12 | 60 % of Base Fees for the remaining months up to month 24, plus any unamortised onboarding fee |
| Months 13 to 24 | 40 % of Base Fees for the remaining months up to month 24 |
| Months 25 to 36 | 10 % of Base Fees for the remaining months of the Initial Term |
| After the Initial Term | None |

11.5 On termination, Crate returns Customer Data under Schedule D and provides reasonable transition assistance for up to 60 days at the consultancy rate.

## 12. Notices

Notices must be in writing and sent by registered post or by email with delivery confirmation to the addresses on the signature page. Notices are deemed received on the second working day after posting or on the next working day after email.

## 13. Governing law and jurisdiction

Spanish law governs this agreement. The courts of Madrid have exclusive jurisdiction.

## 14. General

The agreement, with its Schedules, is the entire agreement between the parties and replaces earlier understandings. Amendments must be in writing and signed. If a clause is held invalid, the others remain in force. In the event of conflict, the order of precedence is: Schedule D (data protection), this agreement, Schedule C (service levels), Schedule A, Schedule B.
