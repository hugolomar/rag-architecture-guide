# Bonus and Performance Plan 2026

**Version:** 2026.1 · **Plan year:** 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 · **Classification:** Restricted. People Team and executive leadership only

## 1. Rating scale

| Rating | Description | Target share of population |
|---|---|---|
| 5: Exceptional | Clearly exceeds objectives and raises the performance of the team | 5 % |
| 4: Exceeds expectations | Consistently exceeds objectives | 20 % |
| 3: Meets expectations | Delivers the objectives | 55 % |
| 2: Partially meets | Delivers part of the objectives | 15 % |
| 1: Does not meet | Below the level required | 5 % |

The shares are a guide for calibration, not quotas for individual teams.

## 2. Target bonus by band

| Band | Target bonus (% of base salary) |
|---|---|
| B1 and B2 | 5 % |
| B3 | 8 % |
| B4 | 12 % |
| B5 | 18 % |
| B6 | 25 % |

The band corresponds to the salary band in the Salary Bands 2026 document.

## 3. Payout multiplier by rating

| Rating | Multiplier on target bonus |
|---|---|
| 5 | 150 % |
| 4 | 120 % |
| 3 | 100 % |
| 2 | 50 % |
| 1 | 0 % |

The maximum payout is 150 % of target.

## 4. Company performance gate

The bonus pool depends on company results. If annual EBITDA is below **85 %** of budget, the pool is reduced by **50 %**; if it is below 70 %, no bonus is paid for bands B1 to B3 and the pool for B4 to B6 is reduced by 75 %. If EBITDA is above 110 % of budget, the pool may be increased by up to 20 % at the discretion of the board.

## 5. Eligibility

1. The employee must have worked at least **6 months** during the plan year.
2. The employee must be employed and not under notice at the payment date (**31 March 2027**).
3. The employee must not have received a serious or very serious disciplinary sanction in the plan year.
4. Employees on unpaid leave for more than 60 days are paid pro rata.

### 5.1 Pro rata for part-year employment

The bonus is pro-rated to the months worked in the plan year. An employee who joins on 1 July 2026 with a base salary of EUR 50,000 and a rating of 3 (band B3):

- Target bonus = 8 % x EUR 50,000 = EUR 4,000
- Multiplier for rating 3 = 100 %
- Pro rata = 6 / 12
- Bonus = EUR 4,000 x 100 % x 6 / 12 = **EUR 2,000**

### 5.2 Leavers

Employees who resign or are dismissed for cause before the payment date forfeit the bonus. Employees whose employment ends through redundancy, retirement or death receive a pro-rated bonus at the target multiplier (100 %).

## 6. Worked example

A band B3 employee with a base salary of EUR 50,000 and a rating of 4, in a year when the company meets its EBITDA budget:

- Target bonus = 8 % x EUR 50,000 = EUR 4,000
- Multiplier for rating 4 = 120 %
- Bonus = EUR 4,000 x 120 % = **EUR 4,800**

If EBITDA were 80 % of budget, the pool would be reduced by 50 % and the bonus would be EUR 2,400.

## 7. Calendar

| Milestone | Date |
|---|---|
| Self-assessment | By 31 January |
| Manager assessment | By 15 February |
| Calibration between directors | Last week of February |
| Results communicated | First half of March |
| Bonus payment | 31 March |

## 8. Disputes

An employee who disagrees with their rating may ask for a review by the next-level manager and the People Team within 10 working days of being told the result. The outcome of the review is final within the plan.
