# Privacy Policy

**Version:** 3.0 · **Effective:** 2025-05-01 · **Controller:** Crate Expectations S.L. · **Contact:** privacy@crateexpectations.example

## 1. Who we are

Crate Expectations S.L. ("Crate", "we") is the controller of the personal data collected through its website and its marketing activities. For personal data that customers upload to our platform, Crate acts as a **processor** on the customer's behalf; that processing is governed by the customer's data processing agreement and not by this policy.

## 2. What we collect and why

| Category | Examples | Purpose | Legal basis | Retention |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Contact data | Name, work email, company, phone | Answering enquiries and sales follow-up | Legitimate interest | 12 months after last contact |
| Account data | Username, email, hashed password, role | Providing the service | Performance of contract | Contract term plus 5 years |
| Billing data | Billing address, tax ID, invoice history | Tax and accounting duties | Legal obligation | 6 years |
| Usage and log data | IP address, timestamps, pages visited | Security and service improvement | Legitimate interest | 13 months |
| Analytics cookies | Pseudonymous identifiers | Audience measurement | Consent | 13 months |
| Recruitment data | CV, interview notes | Evaluating job applicants | Pre-contractual steps; consent for talent pool | 6 months (24 months if the candidate joins the talent pool) |

### 2.1 Special categories

We do not intentionally collect special category data (health, beliefs, biometrics). If you send such data to us unprompted, we will delete it unless we are legally required to keep it.

## 3. Cookies

| Cookie type | Purpose | Consent required |
|---|---|---|
| Strictly necessary | Session management, load balancing, security | No |
| Preferences | Language and region settings | Yes |
| Analytics | Measuring which pages are used | Yes |
| Marketing | Not used | n/a |

You can change your cookie choices at any time from the "Cookie settings" link in the footer. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing carried out before withdrawal.

## 4. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We share it with:

- **Infrastructure providers** for hosting and backups, under processor agreements.
- **Analytics providers**, only if you have accepted analytics cookies.
- **Professional advisers** (lawyers, auditors) under confidentiality duties.
- **Authorities**, where required by law.

All processing takes place within the European Economic Area. If a transfer outside the EEA becomes necessary, we use standard contractual clauses and carry out a transfer impact assessment.

## 5. Your rights

You can exercise the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection by writing to privacy@crateexpectations.example.

- We respond within **one month**; for complex requests we may extend by up to two further months and will tell you why.
- We may ask for proof of identity.
- Requests are free unless they are manifestly unfounded or excessive.
- If you are unhappy with our answer, you may complain to the competent supervisory authority.

## 6. Personal data breaches

If a breach is likely to result in a high risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify you without undue delay. We notify the supervisory authority within **72 hours** of becoming aware of a breach, unless it is unlikely to result in a risk to individuals. Where Crate is acting as a processor, we notify the customer within the period set out in the data processing agreement.

## 7. Security

We apply encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access control, logging of access to personal data, and regular penetration testing. Staff with access to personal data are bound by confidentiality duties and receive annual data protection training.

## 8. Children

Our services are not directed at people under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## 9. Changes to this policy

Material changes are announced on this page and, where we hold your email, by email at least 30 days before they take effect.
