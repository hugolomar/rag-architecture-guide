# Service Level Agreement: Crate Platform

**Version:** 1.0 · **Effective:** 2022-01-01 · **Classification:** Confidential

## 1. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Availability** | The percentage of minutes in a calendar month in which the Platform is accessible |
| **Monthly Fee** | The subscription fee payable for the month |

## 2. Availability commitment

Crate commits to a monthly Availability of **99.5 %**. Scheduled maintenance, limited to **8 hours per month**, is excluded from the calculation. Maintenance should be notified at least 48 hours in advance.

## 3. Service credits

| Monthly Availability | Credit (% of Monthly Fee) |
|---|---|
| 99.5 % or above | 0 % |
| Below 99.5 % and at least 98.0 % | 5 % |
| Below 98.0 % | 10 % |

The maximum credit in any month is 10 % of the Monthly Fee. The Customer must request a credit within **15 days** of the end of the affected month.

## 4. Support

Support is provided during working hours only (09:00 to 18:00, Monday to Friday, Madrid time). Round-the-clock support is not included in this agreement.

| Severity | Description | Response time |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Platform unavailable | 2 working hours |
| High | Main functionality degraded | 8 working hours |
| Medium | Defect with a workaround | 2 working days |
| Low | Question | 5 working days |

## 5. Backups

Daily backup with a retention of 14 days. No recovery point or recovery time commitments are made.

## 6. Reporting

Crate provides an availability report once a year on request.

## 7. Exclusions

Force majeure, failures of the Customer's own systems, and use of the Platform contrary to the documentation are excluded from the Availability calculation.
