# Employee Security Guidelines

**Version:** 2.3 · **Effective:** 2025-11-01 · **Owner:** Security Team · **Applies to:** all employees and contractors

## 1. Purpose

These guidelines explain what every person at Crate Expectations S.L. must do to keep company and customer information safe. They are written for non-specialists. Engineers who operate production systems must also follow the more detailed technical standards owned by the Security Team.

## 2. Data classification

| Class | Definition | Examples | Handling |
|---|---|---|---|
| Public | Approved for release | Marketing site, published documentation | No restrictions |
| Internal | For staff only; low impact if leaked | Internal announcements, org charts | Share only within Crate |
| Confidential | Significant impact if leaked | Customer contracts, pricing, source code | Need-to-know access; do not send to personal email |
| Restricted | Severe impact if leaked | Personal data of customers, salary data, security credentials, incident reports | Named access only; encrypted at rest; no copies on laptops or removable media |

If you are unsure of a document's class, treat it as Confidential until the owner confirms.

## 3. Accounts and passwords

1. Use the company password manager; never reuse passwords across services.
2. Passwords generated by the manager must be at least 16 characters.
3. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, VPN, source control and cloud consoles.
4. Never share your credentials, even with colleagues or the IT service desk.
5. Report any request for your password immediately as a phishing attempt.

## 4. Phishing and social engineering

Be suspicious of messages that create urgency, ask for credentials or payments, or come from unexpected senders. To report one, use the "Report phishing" button in your mail client. If you clicked a link or opened an attachment, report it right away; fast reporting limits damage and no blame is attached to honest mistakes.

## 5. Devices

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Corporate devices only | Company data must not be stored on personal phones or computers |
| Full-disk encryption | Mandatory on all laptops; verified by IT |
| Screen lock | Automatic after 5 minutes; lock manually when leaving |
| Updates | Install operating system and application updates within 7 days of release |
| Lost or stolen device | Report to security@crateexpectations.example within **1 hour** of noticing |

## 6. Working remotely

Use the corporate VPN for internal systems, avoid public Wi-Fi without the VPN, and keep screens out of sight in public places. See the Remote Work Policy for the full list of requirements.

## 7. Reporting a security incident

Any suspected incident (lost device, suspicious access, data sent to the wrong recipient) must be reported to **security@crateexpectations.example** or by phone to the security hotline immediately. Do not attempt to investigate or fix the problem yourself, and do not delete anything. The Security Team assesses every report and decides whether it becomes a formal incident under the incident response process.

## 8. Using AI tools

Do not paste Confidential or Restricted information into external AI tools. Only use AI tools approved by the Security Team for work involving company data.

## 9. Consequences

Failure to follow these guidelines may lead to disciplinary action in line with the disciplinary procedure. Reporting honestly and promptly is always taken into account.
